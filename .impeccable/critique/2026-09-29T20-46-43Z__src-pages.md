## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: authored, not templated. One day in one forest (plum dawn → blue noon → amber dusk → firefly twilight), one integer pixel unit (--px) drawing sprites, plate notches, focus ring, index ticks and type shadow alike, and creatures chosen per product (beavers building a home for WordPress.com, the web that keeps gnats for Akismet, fireflies keeping their own light for Gravatar). The least specific element is the trailhead kiosk: the same roofed board and three-item pinned wing at all seven stops, covering about half the desktop frame, which reads as one card component re-skinned by the sky and contradicts DESIGN.md's no-cards/panels rule.

Deterministic scan: `impeccable detect` on src/pages, src/components and src/layouts: 0 findings each. Browser injection: home page 2 × tight-leading (line-height 1.15 on .plate, global.css:246), almost certainly the two "Explore a random biome" plates, whose text length is padded by the visually hidden ", opens one product's website". Judged a mostly-false positive (single-line bold button labels, not running text), with one real check: 1.15 leading will look cramped if that label wraps on narrow screens. /wander/ (no-JS view) and 404: clean.

## Overall Impression

A genuinely authored world with a strong arc and robust fallbacks. The weakest part is what holds the words: one repeated kiosk. The most urgent issue is a real interaction bug: the forest's clickable creatures only work at one exact scroll position per clearing.

## What's Working

1. One grid for everything: --px drives plate notches, hard drops, ticks and the gold focus ring, so the UI is drawn into the world.
2. Scroll is time: each clearing at its own hour gives the page one dawn-to-dusk story instead of seven sections.
3. The jump is honest and robust: no-repeat pick, timeout race, named status, bfcache reset, working no-JS /wander/ list and plain-link fallbacks.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] Forest clicks only work at one exact scroll position per clearing (user-reported, reproduced). engine.ts locate() treats the treeline wall's passage as spanning the whole distance between clearings (trees ± half a screen), and each stop is exactly one screen tall, so a clearing is "alone" only when its top sits exactly at the viewport top. tick() drops any tap when two clearings are active, and the hover cursor is gated the same way. Measured at 1440×900 on WooCommerce: 72/600 hot points at exact alignment, 0/600 at ±1, ±40, ±150, +300px. Anchor jumps (index ticks, "Walk the trail") and some reloads land exactly aligned, which is why it "sometimes needs a reload" or a particular scroll direction. Fix: route a tap (and the hover test) to whichever clearing is under the pointer: left of the wall's midpoint is clearing a, right is clearing b (the renderer already splits the frame at span.mid), and only swallow taps that land on the dense trunks themselves. Command: /impeccable harden.
2. [P1] The kiosk is one repeated panel at all seven stops, ~half the desktop frame, hiding the scenes (the WordPress.com beavers are a dot at the far right). Fix: a sign native to each clearing (plank on the oak, slab at the falls, board hung from the web's anchor branch), drop or vary the pinned wing, shrink the board to the copy. Command: /impeccable distill, then /impeccable bolder.
3. [P1] The random jump leaves the site without visible warning or a way back. Fix: visible caption ("to one of 7 product sites"), status anchored near the deer/sign, Esc or second click cancels during the run. Command: /impeccable clarify.
4. [P2] The stop index is weak: unlabeled faint ticks, 15px hover labels with no backing (unreadable over VIP's pale rock), ticks overlap the kiosk edge on mobile (x≈383/390), and the nav follows the footer in Tab order. Fix: night-veil plate behind labels, inset from the board on mobile, move the nav before main. Command: /impeccable polish.
5. [P2] The 404 and no-JS /wander/ are orphaned from the world: bare sky, no masthead, one button, no explanation. Fix: masthead plus a still clearing, list the 7 products on the 404, one explanatory line on /wander/. Command: /impeccable delight.
